# Content Rules

## Adapt by Industry

For each hotspot, connect it to the user industry through one of these bridges:

- workflow: what steps should people follow?
- risk: what mistake does the hotspot reveal?
- tool use: what tool or process helps?
- comparison: old way vs new way
- case: how a real scenario would be handled
- checklist: what to verify before acting
- myth-busting: what people misunderstand

## Avoid Template Feel

Across posts, vary:

- opening sentence
- paragraph rhythm
- list length
- examples
- image scene
- title structure
- call-to-think ending

Do not write every post as:

1. "This hotspot shows..."
2. "OpenClaw can..."
3. "Hermes can..."
4. "So..."

## Sales and Compliance

Use the `offer_policy` field.

If direct sales are not allowed:

- do not write "contact us for installation"
- do not promise guaranteed outcomes
- do not imply official partnership unless verified

Use educational framing:

- "this shows a workflow idea"
- "this can be understood as..."
- "a useful way to think about it is..."
- "the key is not X, but Y"

## Body Copy Shape

Recommended Douyin note body:

- 80 to 220 Chinese characters for concise posts
- 2 to 5 short paragraphs
- include the hotspot phrase or a close paraphrase
- include the industry bridge
- include brand terms only when profile requires them

Tags:

- 3 to 8 tags
- include industry tags
- include brand terms only if profile requires them
- avoid stuffing unrelated hot keywords
